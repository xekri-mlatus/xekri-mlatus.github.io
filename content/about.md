Hi, I'm Neko. I write software for a living and notes for fun.

This site is my notebook in public. It's less a blog than a garden: I publish
ideas early, come back to them, and rewrite them as I learn more. If a note looks
unfinished, it probably is.

## What you'll find here

- **Code** — snippets and small lessons I don't want to learn twice, like my
  [shell snippets](/notes/shell-snippets/).
- **Writing** — how I take notes and keep a writing habit, with the occasional
  [honest log](/notes/writing-log-2026/) of how it's going.
- **Walks** — trip reports with maps, such as [a weekend in the hills](/notes/weekend-in-the-hills/).
- **Books** — what I'm [reading](/notes/reading-list/) and what stuck.

## About this site

It's built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the
[PaperMod](https://github.com/adityatelange/hugo-PaperMod) theme, and hosted on
GitHub Pages. There's a longer write-up in
[how this site is built](/notes/how-this-site-is-built/).

## Get in touch

The easiest way to reach me is on [GitHub](https://github.com/neko-in-the-sky).
If you spot a mistake in a note, use the **Suggest a change** link under it.
