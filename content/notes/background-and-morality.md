---
title: "Background is not a norm"
date: 2025-04-23T19:02:30+03:00
tags: [philosophy, gender]
---

People often conflate one's background with normative morality. The latter is about where I must go. The former is about where I've come from and how it has shaped me.

Consider one's gender, for example. My background is male, and it definitely has affected my views, habits and values. It also influences my choices, and I must not be in denial about that. On the contrary, I must strive to get more awareness about its effects.

But never, ever should I use a syllogism of a form "All true men do X. I'm a man. Therefore I must do X". That is a logical abomination that so many fall victim to - myself included.
