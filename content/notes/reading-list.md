## Read this year

| Book | Author | Verdict |
| ---- | ------ | ------- |
| *Walden* | Henry David Thoreau | Slower than I expected, and better for it |
| *Meditations* | Marcus Aurelius | One page a morning works best |
| *A Pattern Language* | Christopher Alexander | Changed how I look at rooms and streets |
| *The Elements of Style* | William Strunk Jr. | Short, strict, still useful |

## Reading now

**_How Buildings Learn_ by Stewart Brand.** It's about how buildings change after
they're finished. A lot of it applies to notes, too: the useful ones are the
ones that keep getting adapted.

## Up next

1. *The Living Mountain* — Nan Shepherd
2. *Bird by Bird* — Anne Lamott
3. *The Timeless Way of Building* — Christopher Alexander

## How I choose

I mostly follow threads: a book mentions another book, and that one goes on the
list. *A Pattern Language* led to *How Buildings Learn*, which led to almost
everything in "Up next".
