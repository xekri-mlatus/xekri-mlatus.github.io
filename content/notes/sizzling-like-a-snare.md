title: "Sizzling like a snare"
date: 2025-04-21T19:14:51+03:00
tags: [music]
---

Today, for the first time in my life, I heard a sound that Lana Del Rey once described as 'telephone wires above are sizzling like a snare' in her Summertime Sadness. Though in my case, it wasn’t telephone wires but a power transmission line. Having been born and raised in a city, I’d never been close enough to power lines to hear them humming before. This experience boosted my mood, even if only for a little while.
